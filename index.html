The research problem

Good skin care should not depend on skin tone, location or cost.

In Sri Lanka many people avoid professional advice because of low awareness and the high cost of imported drugs. Existing digital tools analyse flat 2D images, ignore climate triggers such as UV, humidity and pollution, and perform poorly on Fitzpatrick V–VI skin. Read the domain analysis →

Integrated architecture of the four components and the Progressive Learning Loop.

How it connects

Diagnose → Treat → Track → Learn

1. Understand

Photos, profile and local climate data become a Comprehensive Skin Profile.

2. Treat

The profile drives a personalised, simulated herbal treatment plan.

3. Support

Adherence tracking and a safe community keep users on course.

4. Improve

Outcomes and moderator feedback retrain the models continuously.
